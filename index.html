How we build a tool

Four rules we set ourselves, every time we start a new tool.

  • Calculate first

    The calculation model comes before the design. We test it against real measurements before anyone sees it.

  • Little input

    Clicks instead of forms. No file uploads and no account to create.

  • An honest answer

    If you are already in good shape, the tool says so. No advice just to sell something.

  • One thing, done well

    Each tool answers one question. Whatever does not belong to it, we leave out on purpose.

Harborlight: where we are now

Harborlight is a desktop app that brings your Zabbix alerts together in one clear place. You get your own alert panel with tabs per host group, severity filters, per-host exceptions and a snooze button for when you need a moment without alerts. No noise from separate Windows notifications, just one calm overview of what is really going on.

What we pay attention to

  • Read-only access

    Harborlight reads from Zabbix and changes nothing there. Your settings and exceptions are stored locally on your own computer.

  • Open source

    The source code of the basic version will be public. That way you can see for yourself what a tool with access to your monitoring actually does.

  • Your AI, your data

    In the pro version, Harborlight gives a short explanation of what is going on with an alert. For that you connect your own AI, such as Claude, OpenAI, Azure OpenAI or a local model. Your data does not have to leave your own environment.
