How we build an app or tool

Four rules we set ourselves, for everything we build.

  • Calculate first

    The calculation model comes before the design. We test it against real measurements before anyone sees it.

  • Little input

    Clicks instead of forms. No file uploads and no account to create.

  • An honest answer

    If you are already in good shape, we say so. No advice just to sell something.

  • One thing, done well

    Each app or tool answers one question. Whatever does not belong to it, we leave out on purpose.

Harborlight: where we are now

Harborlight is a desktop app that brings your Zabbix alerts together in one clear place. You get your own alert panel with tabs per host group, severity filters, per-host exceptions, a pop-up for each new problem and a snooze button for when you need a moment without alerts. Harborlight also looks ahead: it shows which problems are likely to occur in the coming week. No noise from separate Windows notifications, just one calm overview of what is really going on.

What we pay attention to

  • Read-only by default

    Harborlight reads from Zabbix and changes nothing there. Acknowledging a problem from the app only works once you switch it on yourself. Your settings and exceptions are stored locally on your own computer.

  • Works in closed networks

    The license key is checked offline on your own computer. Harborlight does not need to reach us to keep working.

  • Your AI, your data

    In the Pro version, Harborlight will give a short explanation of what is going on with an alert. For that you connect your own AI, such as Claude, OpenAI, Azure OpenAI or a local model. Your data does not have to leave your own environment.
